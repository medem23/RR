RodentRich: A Benchmark for Rodent Tracking in Enriched Environments

Paper (Coming Soon) Code (Coming Soon) Data (Coming Soon)

Abstract

Rodents are central to neuroscience and preclinical research, where non-invasive video-based monitoring enables quantitative analysis of behavior and experimental outcomes. Yet, no comparable multi-object tracking (MOT) benchmark exists for rodent tracking, in contrast to resources such as MOT17 for human tracking. To fill this gap, we present RodentRich, a curated dataset comprising 34 minutes of multi-rodent recordings across 30 sequences ranging from 30 seconds to 5 minutes, encompassing approximately 60,000 annotated frames. The dataset covers diverse environmental enrichments, lighting conditions, and challenging scenarios, including prolonged occlusions, close-proximity interactions, and complex social dynamics. We further benchmark state-of-the-art trackers on RodentRich, establishing extensive baselines for reproducibility. RodentRich thus provides a realistic benchmark designed to drive advances in computer vision for multi-object tracking and to facilitate automated behavioral analysis in animal research, ultimately supporting both biomedical advancement and animal welfare.
